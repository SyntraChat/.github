# Community conduct

Be respectful and specific. Critique code and ideas, not people. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing and sharing private conversations without consent are unacceptable.
Respect boundaries and stop unwanted contact. Maintainers may remove content, restrict
participation or ban repeated offenders, proportionate to the behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to an organization administrator through an available
GitHub contact. A dedicated reporting contact must be designated before broad community
launch. Maintainers must keep reports confidential where possible and recuse themselves
when directly involved in a dispute.
